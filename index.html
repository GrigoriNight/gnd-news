Stuck?

Support

The game won't start, or files look corrupted

Open the launcher, go to Support and choose Verify game files. It re-checks your install and re-downloads anything broken, without a full reinstall.

I can't sign in

Use the account button in the launcher to sign in or register. If you've forgotten your password, Forgot password? on the login form emails you a reset link. Make sure you're using the email address you verified.

I found a bug

Report it from Support → Report a bug in the launcher. Your account and device details attach automatically, which is usually what's needed to reproduce it — so in-launcher reports get fixed faster than emails.

I think I was banned by mistake

Open Support → Appeal a ban in the launcher and tell us what happened. Appeals are reviewed by a person, and you'll get a reply at your account email.

Is the game free?

Yes — GrigoriNightDragon is free to play. There's an optional in-game store, and some items are limited edition: one per account, for life.

How do I reach a human?

Email GrigoriNightDragon@gmail.com. Bug reports and ban appeals sent from inside the launcher land in the same place, with the diagnostics already attached.
